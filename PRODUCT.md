# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Workspace members managing document uploads and processing during everyday work.
The dashboard is a task-focused authenticated surface, not a marketing site.

## Product Purpose

Vkit Orbit provides a document-processing workspace. The installed product supports
plain-text and Markdown documents; runtime integration availability must not be
inferred from schema, fixtures, or typechecks.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, functional. Preserve the existing identity and semantic palette owned
by `packages/brand`. Universe is a reference for shell composition and interaction,
not a replacement brand or a source of new product capabilities.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard chrome, invented metrics, nonfunctional search or
notification controls, and copied Universe branding.

## Design Principles

- Keep navigation predictable and preserve native link semantics.
- Distinguish product identity, workspace context, and account identity.
- Separate presentation from fetching, authentication, and routing.
- Offer only supported actions; do not disguise placeholders as working controls.
- Preserve the current product scope while improving its shell and root-level URLs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible control labels, responsive
navigation, and reduced motion. Do not claim exhaustive WCAG compliance without
an audit.
